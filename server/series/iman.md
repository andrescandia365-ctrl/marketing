# IMAN

Tagline: Números claros. Local que crece.

## What IMAN is
A till and store-management app for Argentine corner stores ("kioscos" and "almacenes"). Sold as a barcode laser scanner plus a license (12, 24 or 36 months).

## Works today (only these may be shown as real)
- Sell at the counter by scanning; prints the ticket.
- Stock, expiry dates ("vencimientos"), supplier orders ("pedidos").
- Buy by the case, sell by the unit (packs); returns to customers and to suppliers.
- Close the cash register ("caja") with the monthly cash sheet; the owner sees the month's profit in a private owner view protected by a PIN.
- Works without internet; the "Sincronizar" button syncs.
- A phone companion that checks the store: Vender, Stock, Llegó (the delivery arrived), Vence (what's expiring), Dueño. The phone never charges customers.
- Several stores under one account.

## The story we tell
IMAN is a LOOP someone can fold into their daily routine, and that loop is the product. The daily loop:
open the store → sell at the counter → the delivery arrives ("Llegó") → check what's expiring ("Vence") → order from suppliers before the cutoff → close the cash register → Sincronizar → the owner checks the numbers.
Each step fixes one pain.

## The Bad
The chaos of the store, personified: stock that doesn't match the shelf, the paper notebook, the yogurt that expired Tuesday, the supplier cutoff missed by ten minutes, the cash register that never squares, the owner who can't take a Sunday off.

## The Good
The loop itself: calm, dependable, the sane voice in the chaotic store. Not a robot: a routine that works.

## Who watches
Owners and employees of kioscos and almacenes in Argentina.
